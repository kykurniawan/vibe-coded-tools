🔤 Case Converter

Convert text between lowercase, UPPERCASE, Title Case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, and kebab-case.

Case Types

camelCase: first word lowercase, subsequent words capitalized, no separators

PascalCase: all words capitalized, no separators

snake_case: all lowercase, underscores between words

kebab-case: all lowercase, hyphens between words

CONSTANT_CASE: all uppercase, underscores between words
