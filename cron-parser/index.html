⏰ Cron Expression Parser

Parse cron expressions into a human-readable schedule and compute the next run times. Supports standard 5-field and 6-field (seconds) syntax. 100% client-side — your expressions never leave the browser.

5-field format: minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week · 6-field adds seconds first

⚠️ Notes: When both day-of-month and day-of-week are restricted, a day matches if either field matches (standard cron OR rule). L is supported for day-of-month (last day of month) and day-of-week (Saturday). W and # are not supported yet. Day-of-week accepts 0–7 (both 0 and 7 = Sunday) and 3-letter names (SUN–SAT, JAN–DEC).
